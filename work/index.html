04 / THE METHOD

No deck that ends
at the handover.

  1. Diagnose. Understand the business, bottleneck, and founder dependency.
  2. Architect. Decide what needs ownership, visibility, a workflow, or a different decision.
  3. Build. Create the trackers, SOPs, dashboards, AI workflows, GTM systems, or investor materials.
  4. Execute. Move decisions through follow-up, coordination, and review.
  5. Handover. Leave a system the team can actually run.
